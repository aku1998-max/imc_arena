Help for parents and students using the Math Challenge app.

Contact us: support@mathchallenge.in.th

Tell us the device you use and what happened. We usually reply within 2 working days.

Common questions

How do I set up the app for my child?

Install the app, tap "I'm a parent — get started", sign in with your email and the code we send, give consent, then create your child's profile with a nickname and grade. Tap "Start child mode" and hand the phone over.

What is free and what is in the Full plan?

The daily challenge (5 questions a day with explanations) and 7 days of progress are free. The Full plan adds practice by topic, retrying mistakes and 30 days of progress. The price is shown in the app before you buy.

How do I cancel the Full plan?

Subscriptions are managed by your app store. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

I bought the Full plan but it isn't unlocked

Open the parent area, choose your child and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple or Google account you paid with. If it still doesn't work, email us.

My child found a mistake in a question

After answering, tap "Report a problem with this question". Our question team reviews every report.

How do I delete our data?

In the parent area, choose your child and use "Delete profile". This removes the profile and all practice history. See the privacy policy for details.
