Daily challenge

A fresh set of five, every single day

Each day you get five new questions picked for your grade: two easy ones to warm up, two medium, and one harder one to stretch you.

  • Takes about six minutes, so it fits before school or after dinner.
  • Pick an answer, then tap Check answer. You can change your mind before you check.
  • Pictures zoom in with a tap, so you can see every detail of a shape or chart.
  • Always free, for every student.

Explanations

Right or wrong, you always learn why

After every answer, the elephant shows you the working. If you got it right, you see why. If not, you see how to get there, step by step.

  • Every answer is marked with a tick or a cross and the words "Correct" or "Not quite", never just a colour.
  • A short, friendly explanation written for your grade.
  • Spot a mistake in a question? Tap Report a problem and the question team takes a look.

✓36 cm²Correct
C16 cm²
Not quite. Here's how A square has 4 equal sides, so each side is 24 ÷ 4 = 6 cm. Area = 6 × 6 = 36 cm².
