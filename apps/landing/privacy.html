Privacy policy

Math Challenge app · Last updated 27 September 2026

In short: we collect as little as we can. Children use a nickname only. There are no ads, no chat, no public profiles and no tracking for advertising. A parent or guardian controls the account and can export or delete it at any time.

Who we are

The Math Challenge app ("the app") is a maths practice app for students in grades 4–6, made by the organisers of the Math Challenge (IMC) competitions. Contact: support@mathchallenge.in.th.

What we collect

About the parent or guardian

  • Email address, used only to sign in with a one-time code and to send account messages.
  • A record that you gave consent for your child, with the date and policy version.
  • Subscription status if you buy the Full plan. Payment is handled by Apple or Google; we never see card details.

About the child

  • A nickname (not a real name), an avatar choice, school grade, language and time zone.
  • Practice activity: which questions were answered, whether answers were correct, and time taken.
  • Problem reports the child sends about a question (up to 1,000 characters).

We do not collect a child's real name, birth date, school, photos, location, contacts, microphone or camera. The app contains no advertising or third-party tracking tools.

How we use it

  • To run the app: choose questions, check answers, show explanations and track progress.
  • To show the parent a summary of their child's practice.
  • To check subscription status and unlock the Full plan.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems, using logs that do not contain answers or sign-in codes.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help run the app, under contract and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage).
  • Render (app servers).
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google (subscriptions).

Data is stored in Singapore. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Practice history is kept while the account is active.
  • When a parent deletes a child profile, the profile and all of its practice history are deleted.
  • Data exports are deleted after 7 days. Expired sign-in sessions are removed after 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See and export your child's data from the parent area of the app.
  • Delete a child profile, or your whole account, from the parent area. Deleting does not cancel a store subscription; cancel it in your Apple or Google account.
  • Correct details such as nickname or grade in the parent area.
  • Email us to ask about your data or to make a complaint. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Children

A parent or guardian must set up the account and give consent before a child can use the app. Children cannot make purchases, change settings or open the parent area without the parent's email sign-in.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Sign-in details on the device are kept in the phone's secure storage. Access to data is limited to staff who need it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell parents in the app before the change applies.
